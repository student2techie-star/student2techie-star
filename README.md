## 🏢 About Us

**Student2Techie** is a student-first tech company on a mission to eliminate the *"no experience, no job — no job, no experience"* paradox that traps thousands of college graduates every year.

We design **structured, industry-grade internship programs** that expose students to real-world development environments — not just tutorials and theory. From day one, interns contribute to live projects, collaborate with senior engineers, and build portfolios that actually impress recruiters.

> 🎯 **Our Mission:** To empower every college student — regardless of their college tier or background — with the technical skills, industry exposure, and professional confidence needed to launch a successful tech career.

## 📋 Eligibility

<details>
<summary><strong>📌 Click to view eligibility criteria</strong></summary>

<br/>

✅ **Who can apply?**

- Students currently enrolled in **1st, 2nd, or 3rd year** of any undergraduate program (BCA, B.Tech, B.Sc CS, BBA IT, etc.)
- **Any branch** is welcome — we care about your curiosity, not just your department
- Basic familiarity with at least **one programming language** or a basic understanding of the web (HTML/CSS is enough!)
- Students from **any college, any tier, anywhere in India**
- Both **beginners and intermediate learners** are encouraged to apply

❌ **Who should NOT apply?**

- Students looking for a passive experience with no commitment
- Final year students with less than **4 weeks** available before placement season

</details>
